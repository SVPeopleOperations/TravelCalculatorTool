Key policy rules this calculator applies
Personal vehicle is recommended for trips 200 miles or less one-way
Flights are preferred for trips 300+ miles one-way
Lodging is reimbursable when your destination is 90+ miles from home
Airfare must be booked at least 14 days in advance per policy
Groups of 3 or more must share a single rental vehicle
All flights and rental cars must be booked through Navan
Mileage rate covers gas, oil, and wear — no fuel receipts needed
Expenses of $500 or more require written approval before booking
